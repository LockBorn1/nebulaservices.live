FAQs

Frequently asked

How do I request media library access?

Use the Access page and submit the access request form with your preferred package. That form is only for new library applications — not general support.

How long does approval take?

Reviews are manual. Incomplete or low-effort requests may be declined without follow-up. If approved, you receive credentials by email.

I was approved but Cloudflare Access denies me

Sign in with the exact email listed in your approval letter. A different inbox will fail even if the Jellyfin password is correct. Wait a few minutes after approval if the allow list has not updated yet.

Jellyfin login fails after Access succeeds

Use the username and password from the approval email. If approval was very recent, wait 5–10 minutes and try again. Still stuck? Send a support message with the email you used (not your password).

What is WARP / Cloudflare One for?

Optional on desktop; useful on mobile or restricted networks. Join organization nebulaservices with your approved email. Full steps are on Instructions.

What do the paid plans change?

All approved accounts can stream existing library titles. Paid tiers add a monthly request quota for shows/movies that may be added to the shared library after review. See Services & Plans.

Can I share my account?

No. Credential sharing is grounds for termination. We have methods in place to detect it. See Legal.

How do I change my plan or password?

Email admin or use the support form below. Include the email on your account and what you need changed. Do not send passwords in plain chat outside official channels.

Where is system status?

Check the Status page for live platform health when available.
