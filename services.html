Jellyfin library

Stream from a private Jellyfin instance in the browser or official apps. Libraries are curated and shared across approved users — new titles from request tiers are added here after review, not delivered as personal files.

◈

Cloudflare Access

Before Jellyfin loads, Cloudflare checks that your email is on our allow list. Only addresses we add after approval can pass. A different inbox will be denied even if you know a Jellyfin password.

◎

Cloudflare One (WARP)

Optional on desktop; useful on phones and restricted networks. Join organization nebulaservices with your approved email so library traffic follows our Zero Trust path. Step-by-step setup is on Instructions.

▣

What this is not

Access is media-library only. It does not include shell access, admin panels, or control of infrastructure. Support and account changes go through the admin email, not shared chats.
