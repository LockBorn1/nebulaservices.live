Platform

Core services

Everything below is for approved accounts only. There is no public self-serve signup.

✧

Jellyfin library

Stream from a private Jellyfin instance in the browser or official apps. Libraries are curated and shared across approved users. New titles from request tiers are added to this shared collection after review — not delivered as personal downloads.

◈

Cloudflare Access

Before Jellyfin loads, Cloudflare checks that your email is on our allow list. Only addresses we add after approval can pass. A different inbox will be denied even if you know a Jellyfin password.

◎

Cloudflare One (WARP)

Optional on desktop; useful on phones and restricted networks. Join organization nebulaservices with your approved email so library traffic follows our Zero Trust path. Full setup steps are on Instructions.

▣

Manual review & support

Access is media-library only. It does not include shell access, admin panels, or control of infrastructure. Account changes and support go through the Support Center or admin email.

Requests

How media requests work

Shared, not personal

Approved requests are added to the shared library when capacity allows. They are not private downloads or exclusive to one account.

Review required

Every request is reviewed. Incomplete, duplicate, or low-effort requests may be declined. Quotas reset monthly; unused slots do not roll over unless we say otherwise.
