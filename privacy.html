What we collect when you use the site or request access, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit an access request we collect the details you provide on the form (name, email, preferred credentials, package choice, and your written reasons). Support form messages collect name, email, topic, and message. Server and edge logs may include IP address, browser type, and timestamps when you visit the site or use Access-protected services.

How we use it

Form data is used to review access requests, create library accounts, handle support, and contact you about approval status. Logs are used for security, abuse detection, and reliability.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers we use (for example form delivery, email, and Cloudflare Access).

Retention

Request and account records are kept while needed to operate the service and handle support. You may use the Support Center to ask about deletion of request data.
